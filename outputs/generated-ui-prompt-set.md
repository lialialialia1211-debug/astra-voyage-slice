# Generated UI Prompt Set

- Generation mode: built-in ImageGen
- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Output use: original science-fantasy browser RPG environment backgrounds
- Shared visual system: ivory human civilization, deep navy, aged brass, restrained cyan illumination, small warning-red accents
- Shared exclusions: no people, no characters, no creatures, no text, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no trademarks, no watermark, no embedded UI, no imitation of an existing game franchise

## `bg_title_harbor`

Original science-fantasy title background showing dawn over a monumental coastal harbor where human civilization begins its journey toward ocean exploration and the stars. Ivory sea walls, navy expedition vessels, brass observatory structures, distant launch spires, and a faint celestial arc. Wide 16:9 establishing view with a strong central architectural focal point; outer edges and lower center remain dark and UI-safe.

## `bg_hub_harbor`

Active expedition harbor concourse linking a terrestrial coastal city to ocean research docks and orbital navigation. Ivory terraces, navy canopies, brass instruments, cyan route lights, distant sea, and launch towers. Wide eye-level view with a broad open center foreground and edge architecture framing the scene.

## `bg_recruit_observatory`

Elegant coastal observatory chamber used to recruit an interplanetary expedition crew. Enormous circular astrolabe rings, ceiling opening to blue sky and faint stars, polished ivory floor, navy wall panels, brass mechanisms, and cyan energy motes. Symmetrical interior with an upper-center focal apparatus and a clean lower center for card-reveal effects.

## `bg_loadout_console`

Interior of a human expedition vessel armory and navigation console. Ivory composite bulkheads, dark navy equipment bays, brushed brass rails, cyan holographic light strips, and a subtle ocean view. Straight-on composition with intentionally low-contrast central wall planes for the main-hand panel and 3×3 equipment matrix.

## `bg_battle_land`

Coastal expedition defense perimeter where an ivory harbor meets windswept cliffs and ancient survey pylons. Distant sea and early orbital launch infrastructure. Wide low eye-level combat arena, open middle distance for enemies, and a dark low-detail bottom 32% for party command cards.

## `bg_battle_ocean`

Stormy ocean around a human expedition platform above a bioluminescent abyss, with research pylons and broken navigation rings. Wide horizon, open low-detail central 45% for a large Boss, and a dark clean bottom 32% for the party HUD. Deep-ocean cyan glow, navy atmosphere, brass edges, and warning-red lamps.

## `bg_private_cabin`

Warm private cabin aboard an ocean-to-space expedition vessel. Ivory curved walls, navy upholstery, brushed brass fixtures, compact desk, and an observation window showing the dark ocean with faint stars reflected above it. Clear full-height standing area on the left and middle; quiet low-contrast right 35% for relationship UI. Intimate but nonsexual.

## `bg_ending_star_signal`

High-altitude observatory view where a mysterious signal rises from Earth’s ocean and connects to a distant constellation, completing the land-to-sea-to-space transition. Wide symmetrical view, central cyan-gold signal focal point, and a dark simple bottom 22% for ending dialogue.

## Deterministic UI derivatives

The remaining 17 assets use the same palette and material language but are generated by `scripts/build-generated-ui.mjs` as exact SVG-to-WebP derivatives. This preserves symmetry, transparent margins, target dimensions, and stretch-safe button/frame geometry.
